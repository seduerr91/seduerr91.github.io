# Pacific Northwest Weekend Backpacking Loop Ideas Near Issaquah

If you want a Friday-evening-to-Sunday itinerary with real mileage but simple logistics, Washington has several excellent options within driving distance of Issaquah.

I prioritized:

- Loop routes with no car shuttle
- Popular trails where you will regularly see other hikers
- Roughly `45` to `60` total miles
- Straightforward trailhead logistics
- Clear permit requirements

Permit rules, road access, trailhead fees, and trail conditions can change, so treat this as a planning shortlist and confirm current details before heading out.

## Parking and Access Notes

I re-checked the official trailhead pages on `July 27, 2026`. A few notes are worth calling out before you commit to one of these weekends:

- **Spider Gap to Buck Creek Pass:** Phelps Creek Trailhead currently shows an active storm-damaged road closure affecting Chiwawa River Road, and the page notes the final approach is roughly `14` miles of rough dirt road even when access is open.
- **Goat Rocks Loop:** Snowgrass is extremely popular and the Forest Service recommends using Berry Patch as the alternate parking area when Snowgrass fills.
- **Dutch Miller Gap Loop:** The former Dutch Miller Gap Trailhead is no longer accessible by vehicle. Start from Dingford Creek Trailhead instead, and expect Forest Road `56` to be rough enough that `4WD` is strongly recommended.
- **Alpine Lakes Mega Loop:** Rachel Lake Trailhead currently lists the Three Queens Fire closure. As of `July 27, 2026`, Box Canyon Road `4930` and the entire Rachel Lake Trail are closed.
- **Wonderland Mini Loop:** Longmire remains the simplest loop-friendly start on the southwest side of the park, but summer parking and entrance queues can still get busy.
- **Enchantments Traverse or Loop:** Stuart Lake and Snow Lakes are both heavily trafficked. Both pages warn that parking can fill early, and Snow Lakes specifically notes that visitors may have to park miles away.

Many Washington backpackers consider Spider Gap to Buck Creek Pass and White Pass to Pilot Ridge two of the premier permit-free alpine loops in the state.

## Suggested Progression

1. Dutch Miller Gap Loop
2. Spider Gap to Buck Creek Pass
3. White Pass to Pilot Ridge
4. Goat Rocks Loop
5. [PCT Section J overnight](/blog/pct-section-j-gear)
6. Wonderland Loop if you secure a permit

## Key Takeaways

- Skip the Enchantments for spontaneous weekends unless you happen to win the permit lottery.
- Spider Gap offers one of the best combinations of scenery, alpine terrain, and straightforward logistics.
- Goat Rocks feels like a miniature Pacific Crest Trail experience and works especially well for long-mile training.
- Dutch Miller Gap is the closest option to Issaquah and makes a strong first overnight training route.
- White Pass to Pilot Ridge is one of the state's best permit-free alpine weekends.
