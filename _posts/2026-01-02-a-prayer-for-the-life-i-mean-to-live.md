As a child, I learned prayers by heart. This is the one I choose to carry now.

## The Prayer to Know by Heart

Remember the village where I began.<br>
Remember the work that made this man.

Factory nights taught grit and grace.<br>
I earned my path and found my place.

From C to A, I found my way.<br>
Then taught eight hundred, day by day.

Through seven homes, eighty-two lands,<br>
I learned how wide the world expands.

I changed my path and learned to code.<br>
I built real systems, bore the load.

When work fell through, I chose my fight.<br>
Ran fifty K, rebuilt my light.

With Joy and cats, I built my home.<br>
Through love, I never stand alone.

I did hard things with help and grace.<br>
Let thanks and pride share equal space.

Sleep eight full hours; greet the dawn.<br>
Run, ride, row; carry on.

Eat simple food that keeps me strong.<br>
Drink water freely all day long.

No drink or paint shall cloud my sight.<br>
I guard tomorrow every night.

Speak without gossip; keep words kind.<br>
Tell tested truth; keep an open mind.

Name real problems; engineer the way.<br>
Build honest answers every day.

Own every error; make things right.<br>
Keep every promise, dark or light.

Give family more than scraps of time.<br>
Call friends while all our stars still shine.

Show up for sorrow, meals, and cheer.<br>
Practice love while they are here.

Do not compare another's climb.<br>
Give credit freely every time.

Guard health and time; protect attention.<br>
Let calendars prove my intention.

Leave room for laughter, trails, and play.<br>
Not every hour must earn its stay.

Stay curious, grateful, brave, and true.<br>
Begin again; I know what to do.
