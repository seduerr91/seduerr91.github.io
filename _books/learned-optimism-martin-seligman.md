## Summary

Do you believe that your failures are permanent, pervasive, and personal? In *Learned Optimism*, Martin Seligman argues that this pessimistic explanatory style is a learned habit, not an innate trait. By recognizing how you interpret adversity, you can retrain your brain to embrace resilience. Seligman introduces the ABCDE model- Adversity, Belief, Consequence, Disputation, and Energization-to help you challenge negative self-talk. Optimism isn't about ignoring reality; it is about using non-negative thinking to boost your immune system, enhance your career, and protect against depression. Ultimately, you can learn to view setbacks as temporary and specific, empowering you to take action and improve your life.
