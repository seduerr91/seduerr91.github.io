## Summary

The vast wilderness of Washington's Cascade Foothills and the I-90 corridor is accessible to millions, yet requires careful navigation and respect. In *Day Hiking: Snoqualmie Region*, Dan A. Nelson provides a comprehensive guide to over 136 day hikes that stretch from the Puget Sound to the Ellensburg Basin. As the author notes, the region offers 'stunning scenery' and some of the 'richest ecosystems' in the country, all within an hour of Seattle. The book serves as a practical tool for both novice and experienced hikers, featuring difficulty ratings, driving directions, and updates on trail conditions. Nelson, who has logged thousands of research miles, emphasizes that while the trails are a joy, they require responsibility. He addresses critical safety issues, ranging from trailhead crime prevention to wilderness etiquette. For those hiking with dogs, he advocates for strict leashing and preparedness, arguing that people will hike with pets regardless, so they must 'do it responsibly.' The guide also includes detailed logistics such as permit information, camping options, and natural history highlights. By balancing detailed maps with the freedom to explore, Nelson equips readers to discover diverse landscapes-from alpine meadows to deep forested valleys-while ensuring their safety and the preservation of the environment.
