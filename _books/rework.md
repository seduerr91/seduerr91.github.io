## Summary

Rework by Jason Fried and David Heinemeier Hansson is a field manual for building simpler, faster, and more productive businesses. It flies in the face of conventional corporate strategy, arguing that you need less-not more-to get ahead. The authors challenge the start-up culture that values long-term planning, outside investment, and sleepless workweeks. Instead, they advocate for a minimalist approach where you start small, stay lean, and focus only on what is essential. They argue that plans are just guesses, so you should stop guessing and start doing. Momentum fuels motivation, so the most important thing is to begin. The book advises breaking projects down into weekly goals rather than yearly plans, embracing constraints as creative fuel, and accepting that 'good enough is fine' for launch. By iterating quickly and learning from real-world feedback, you can out-teach competitors, under-do them by focusing on clarity, and build a profitable business without burning out.
